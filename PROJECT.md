# AI Implementation Partner — Website Spec (v1)

## Purpose
This website exists to clearly communicate what the business is and is not.

- We do NOT build AI software
- We do NOT sell AI tools
- We are NOT a generic agency

We are the **implementation layer** between:
“AI exists” → “AI actually works inside your business”

The primary goal of the site is to:
1. Build trust
2. Clearly explain the service
3. Drive users to submit the AI Audit intake form

This is a single-page, production-ready marketing site.

---

## Brand Positioning

**Positioning Statement**
> We take proven, off-the-shelf AI tools and implement them end-to-end for busy business owners — so they don’t have to touch a thing.

**Tone**
- Calm
- Competent
- Non-hype
- Direct
- Trustworthy
- No buzzwords

**Audience**
- Service-based small business owners
- Appointment-driven businesses
- Owners who know AI could help but do not want to set it up themselves

### 5. How It Works

**Headline**
> A simple, no-stress process

#### Step 1 — Diagnostic Intake
> You answer a short form about your business, your tools, and what feels broken or inefficient.

#### Step 2 — AI Audit & Setup Plan
> We analyze:
- Where AI can realistically help
- Which off-the-shelf tools fit your business
- What it would cost monthly
- What it would save you in time or revenue
>
> You receive a clear plan — not a pitch deck.

#### Step 3 — We Implement Everything
> If you move forward, we:
- Set up the tools
- Connect them to your existing systems
- Test and optimize
- Train your staff
- Monitor performance
>
> You don’t touch settings, APIs, or dashboards unless you want to.
